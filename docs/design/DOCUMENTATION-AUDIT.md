## 3. Major Architectural & Design Decisions

1. **Apple-Inspired Without Cloning:**
   * Adopted Apple Books spatial discipline, squircle curvature, and frosted glass materials (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), while strictly keeping VibeAudio's custom 66-symbol SVG icon system, zero-storefront ethos, and literary serif title styling (`Newsreader`).
2. **Strict Glass Restraint:**
   * Frosted glass is strictly restricted to floating navigation chrome (topbar and mini-player dock) and modal backdrops. Content cards, book items, and chapter playlists must be crisp, opaque `#FFFFFF` or `#F2F2F7` surfaces.
3. **Light Palette & High-Contrast Typography:**
   * Global canvas anchored at `#F5F5F7` with `#FFFFFF` cards.
   * Text hierarchy enforced at `#1D1D1F` (primary, `>13:1` contrast) and `#6E6E73` (secondary, `>4.5:1` contrast).
   * Brand accent tuned to high-contrast warm terracotta amber (`#E65A00` / `#FF9500`).
4. **Zero Build Step & Zero Dependency Policy Maintained:**
   * The redesign requires no bundlers, transpilers, or node packages. All CSS custom properties and ES modules work natively in modern evergreen browsers.

## 5. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk Identified | Potential Impact | Documented Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **ColorThief Dynamic Theme Contamination** | When playing an audiobook, `ui-player-helpers.js` previously calculated dark background variables (`--theme-bg-1`, `--theme-text`), which would override the light theme with dark text. | Documented in `DOC-IMP-001` and Roadmap Phase 1: Adapt `buildTheme()` in `ui-player-helpers.js` to blend extracted cover art colors into light-mode tints. |
| **Service Worker Stale Cache** | Browser could serve old dark stylesheets from Cache Storage. | Documented in `DOC-IMP-001`: Update Service Worker cache version name during release phase to trigger cache invalidation. |
| **Mini-Player Overlap on Mobile** | Floating dock might obstruct bottom card actions or buttons. | Documented in `DOC-DES-004` and `STITCH-SCR-008`: Enforce `padding-bottom: 120px` on all scrollable view containers. |
